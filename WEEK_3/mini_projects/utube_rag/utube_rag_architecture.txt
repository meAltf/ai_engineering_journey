---------    STEPS | High Level Flow   ------------------

YouTube Video
     ↓
Audio Extraction (yt-dlp)
     ↓
Speech → Text (Whisper)
     ↓
Chunking (with timestamps)
     ↓
Embeddings (MiniLM)
     ↓
Store in Vector DB (Qdrant)
     ↓
User Question
     ↓
Embedding → Search → Context
     ↓
LLM → Answer
     ↓
Show on UI
